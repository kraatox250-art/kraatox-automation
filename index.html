About KRAATOX Automation

KRAATOX Automation is a web-based automation service developed to manage, prepare and publish official digital content for the KRAATOX music project.

The service is designed to centralize part of the social media publishing workflow and simplify the regular distribution of music-related videos, promotional visuals and other official KRAATOX content.

KRAATOX is an independent electronic music project focused on hard techno, industrial and hardcore music.

Service Features

Content preparation

KRAATOX Automation helps prepare visual and video content associated with official KRAATOX music releases and promotional campaigns.

Automated publishing workflow

The service helps manage the publication workflow for authorized social media accounts used by the KRAATOX project.

Video publishing

Videos prepared by KRAATOX Automation can be submitted to supported social media platforms through their official APIs when the required authorization has been granted.

Account authorization

KRAATOX Automation only accesses social media accounts after the account owner explicitly authorizes the connection.

How KRAATOX Automation Works

1. Content preparation

Official KRAATOX images, music, video assets and promotional elements are prepared for publication.

2. Account connection

The authorized account owner connects their supported social media account through the platform's official authentication system.

3. Publication preparation

The content is prepared according to the technical requirements of the selected social media platform.

4. Publishing

After authorization, the service can submit the prepared content through the official API of the selected platform.

TikTok Integration

KRAATOX Automation integrates with TikTok to support the publication of official KRAATOX video content.

TikTok Login Kit

TikTok Login Kit is used to allow an authorized TikTok account owner to securely connect their account to KRAATOX Automation.

TikTok Content Posting API

After the required authorization is granted, KRAATOX Automation uses the TikTok Content Posting API to upload and publish approved video content to the connected TikTok account.

User control and authorization

KRAATOX Automation does not publish content to a TikTok account without prior authorization from the account owner.

TikTok access is used only for the functions authorized by the user and in accordance with TikTok's platform requirements.

Privacy and Data Use

KRAATOX Automation only processes information required to operate the authorized integrations and publishing features.

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to authenticate the authorized account and perform the requested publishing functions.

For more information about how data is handled, please read the KRAATOX Automation Privacy Policy.
